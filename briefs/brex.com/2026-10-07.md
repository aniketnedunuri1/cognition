# Brex — account brief

## Verdict

**Warm** *(assessment)* — Assessment: Brex has live, specific migration work (Micronaut 4 remainder across 500+ Kotlin services, plus test-suite gaps the agents exposed) that maps to Code migration & modernization with fintech proof (Nubank, Itaú). But it is already running that exact workload on self-hosted Cursor agents and an in-house agent platform, so this is a displacement/expansion play, not a greenfield one. Warm rather than Act now because of that competitor rollout. [1][2][3]

Watch for: Scope of the Micronaut 4 remainder and the next framework/GraphQL federation upgrade; signs the platform team wants to stop maintaining its self-hosted agent harness; Capital One standardizing engineering tooling or procurement post-close; any Cursor contract renewal timing.
