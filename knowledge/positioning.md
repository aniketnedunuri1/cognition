# Devin positioning

Source of truth for what we say about Devin. Every claim cites a Cognition-owned source; customer proof lives in `customers.json` and `use_cases.json`.

## What Devin is
- Cognition operates Devin, "the first autonomous software engineer." [src_632ad1ffa8] Devin "plans, writes, tests, and ships production code on its own, working inside your codebase and the tools your team already uses." [src_f4092070fe]
- Delegate complete tasks, from bug fixes to multi-repo migrations; Devin plans, codes, reviews, and ships. [src_9dfab27f2e]
- Runs in the cloud or on the engineer's machine, tests end to end on its own VMs and browser, keeps working when the laptop is closed, and splits big tasks across parallel cloud agents. [src_0d723e92fd]
- Works where the team works: tag @Devin in Slack or Teams; automations pick up bugs, failed CI, and alerts as they land. [src_0d723e92fd] [src_2434e7a526]

## The story
- The world's demand for software far exceeds the capacity to build it: teams postpone upgrades and leave vulnerabilities unaddressed because engineering time is finite. [src_9a4d9ab473]
- Engineers operate more like architects and delegate execution to agents. [src_632ad1ffa8] [src_9a4d9ab473]
- As agents write more code, review becomes the bottleneck; Devin Review addresses it. [src_5d13c40e84]
- Outcomes, not usage: the AI Productivity Guarantee. If Devin delivers less engineering value than the customer pays for, Cognition funds usage up to $10M. Eligibility: enterprise customers deploying Devin Cloud at meaningful scale. [src_28549a6272] [src_d409987391]

## Against assistants and AI editors
- "Cursor is an AI code editor. Devin is an autonomous AI engineer." [src_9dfab27f2e]
- Copilot/Cursor adoption means the category is accepted; Devin is the next step: delegated, parallel, end-to-end work instead of in-editor suggestions. DeNA already had AI coding assistants and found Copilot-style tools left developers doing most of the work. [src_28033ce50d] AI inside went from a 50/50 split with GitHub Copilot to 80-90% of engineering work through Devin. [src_9b3bd87de5]
- One product family: Windsurf is now Devin Desktop, the command center for local and cloud agents (including third-party agents via ACP). Never position Windsurf and Devin as two products. [src_1183a08970]
- Devin Fusion routes across frontier models for frontier-level performance at up to 60% lower cost. [src_eb5085ee3c]

## Enterprise readiness
- Deploy in the customer's VPC; inputs and outputs are the customer's IP; SOC 2 Type 2; audit logs, fine-grained access controls, custom IdP. [src_af7c84983e]
- No training on customer data; ISO 27001; deployment as Enterprise SaaS, dedicated single-tenant SaaS, or customer VPC. [src_84d58730f4]
- Dedicated account team, live onboarding, and an assigned Forward Deployed Engineer. [src_af7c84983e]
- Purchasable through AWS Marketplace. [src_bfc8c893c4]
- Public sector: security program, COBOL/legacy modernization, SBOMs per EO 14028. [src_c92ebeeb28]
