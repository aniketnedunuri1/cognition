| Rule | Scope | Evidence | Added |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open with the specific signal about their work and the pain it likely creates. Never describe Devin first. | global | 1 | 2026-10-08 |
| Explain Devin through one concrete mechanism in a single sentence (e.g. it picks up the ticket, runs the tests, opens a PR) with no product adjectives or claims. | global | 1 | 2026-10-08 |
| Cite one customer proof point with a number, matched to their situation. Within one brief, do not reuse the same proof customer in more than two drafts: pick the closest-fit proof per person so forwarded drafts don't share copy. | global | 1 | 2026-10-08 |
| Close with one low-commitment ask (a short meeting) paired with a concrete next step and an easy timing question. Vary the CTA wording across drafts in the same brief; never end two drafts with the same ask phrased identically. | global | 1 | 2026-10-08 |
| Keep emails to 3 short paragraphs, no bullets, no jargon. Conversational tone with contractions and hedged hypotheses. Vary the phrasing: no opener, hypothesis phrase, or sign-off pattern may repeat verbatim across drafts in the same brief. | global | 1 | 2026-10-08 |
| Drafts in one brief get forwarded internally and compared. They must not share a skeleton: vary the opener, the hypothesis framing, the CTA and the proof point so no two drafts read as the same template with the name swapped. | global | 1 | 2026-10-09 |
| Never quote a contact's own press release, announcement or LinkedIn bio back at them verbatim. Paraphrase and add a take (why it matters, what it likely means for their backlog); verbatim echo reads as a search snippet, not homework. | global | 1 | 2026-10-09 |
| Match the ask to the person's level: strategy owners (heads of AI, VPs of platform) get framed around governance, prioritization and which work goes to agents next, not ticket-level mechanics. | global | 1 | 2026-10-09 |
| If the send note concedes the exec likely won't read LinkedIn, don't invest in the connection note: keep it minimal or mark it deprioritized and put the effort into the email. | persona:economic_buyer | 1 | 2026-10-09 |
